# DLW21HN900SQ2#

## Features
1. Small size and low profile (2.0x1.2x0.9mm).
2. High common mode impedance at high frequency effects excellent noise suppression performance.
3. Various common mode impedance from 67 to 180 ohm can be used, selected depending on noise level and signal frequency.
4. Suitable for differential signal line like USB2.0, IEEE1394 and LVDS, because DLW21H does not provide distortion to high speed signal transmission due to its high coupling. (USB2.0: DLW21HN900SQ2)
