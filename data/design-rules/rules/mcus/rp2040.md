# RP2040 and RP2350

> External flash, USB boot, power supply (3.3V IO + 1.1V core), crystal, ADC reference, battery/low-power design, RP2350 migration.

## Quick Reference

- **RP2040: QFN-56 (7x7mm), 30 GPIO, 4 ADC. RP2350A/RP2354A: QFN-60 (7x7mm), 30 GPIO, 4 ADC. RP2350B/RP2354B: QFN-80, 48 GPIO, 8 ADC.** RP2354 variants have internal 2 MB flash. RP2350A is NOT a drop-in replacement for RP2040 (different pinout despite same 7x7mm size) [3, pp.4-5].
- **RP2040: 3.3V IO + internal 1.1V LDO (100 mA max). RP2350: 3.3V IO + internal 1.1V switching regulator (200 mA max).** The RP2350 regulator requires specific external inductor, caps, and layout -- follow the Pico 2/minimal-design component selection and layout [3, pp.5-8; 6, pp.155-156].
- **Reserve a 10 kohm pull-up from flash CS# to its 3.3V supply.** The internal pull is not guaranteed throughout power-on; Raspberry Pi marks the external pull-up DNF with its chosen W25Q128JVS, so fit it according to the selected flash requirements [1, p.9; 3, p.10].
- **Crystal: Abracon ABM8-272-T3 (12 MHz, 10 pF CL, 50 ohm maximum ESR), 15 pF load caps, 1 kohm in series with XOUT.** This recommendation is tuned for 3.3V IOVDD; changes require testing across temperature [1, pp.9-10, 13; 3, pp.13-14].
- **BOOTSEL: pull QSPI_SS low during reset to enter USB mass storage mode.** Connect via a 1 kohm resistor to a grounded pushbutton; hold it through reset and boot sampling [1, p.9; 3, p.10].

## Design Rules

### Power Supply

- **RP2040 reference power: 3.3V -> IOVDD, VREG_VIN, USB_VDD and ADC_AVDD; VREG_VOUT -> DVDD externally at 1.1V.** Never connect DVDD to 3.3V. The minimal designs use NCP1117-3.3 with 10 uF input/output capacitors; Pico/Pico 2 instead use an RT6150 buck-boost [1, pp.7-8, 13; 3, pp.8-9; 2, §4.4; 4, §5.4].
- **RP2040 regulator: 100 mA maximum, 1.1V nominal; fit 1 uF close to both VREG_VIN and VREG_VOUT.** Add 100 nF at each IOVDD and DVDD pin; the regulator output capacitor does not replace DVDD decoupling. Raspberry Pi uses 0402 ceramic regulator capacitors [1, pp.7-8; 6, pp.151, 155-156].
- **RP2040 supply domains are not interchangeable.** IOVDD and VREG_VIN support nominal 1.8-3.3V; USB_VDD needs nominal 3.3V for USB operation and must still be powered if USB is unused. ADC_AVDD supports nominal 1.8-3.3V but ADC performance is compromised below 2.97V; VREG_VIN must remain powered even with an external core supply [6, pp.151-152].
- **RP2350 internal switching regulator requires precise external components and layout.** Inductor: Abracon AOTA-B201610S3R3-101-T (3.3 uH, 0806/2016 metric) -- this specific part has a polarity marking and comes on reel aligned. Inductor orientation matters: wrong orientation causes magnetic field interference with output cap, degrading regulation. Caps: 4.7 uF 0402 on VREG_VIN (C6), the regulated output after L1 (C7, not directly on VREG_LX), and VREG_AVDD (C9). VREG_AVDD filter: 33 ohm + 4.7 uF RC; that supply draws around 200 uA [3, pp.6-8].

> WARNING: RP2350 internal regulator layout is critical. Follow the Pico 2/minimal-design component placement, inductor orientation and return paths. VREG_PGND must return switching currents without disturbing other ground connections [3, pp.6-8].

- **Pico/Pico 2 board VSYS range: 1.8-5.5V; this is not an RP2040/RP2350 chip supply rating.** A 3.0-4.2V Li-ion cell is usable through the reference power-ORing circuit. Short VSYS to VBUS only when USB is the sole source [2, §4.5; 4, §5.5].
- **Pico/Pico 2 3V3 output: Raspberry Pi recommends external load < 300 mA.** Actual budget depends on MCU load and VSYS voltage [2, §2.1; 4, §3.1].
- **Pico power ORing for battery + USB:** Schottky diode from each source to VSYS (simple but lossy), or P-FET (DMG2305UX, Vt_max = 0.9V, Ron = 100 mohm at 2.5V Vgs) gated by VBUS. VBUS disconnects the battery through the P-FET gate. For a low minimum battery voltage or slowly falling VBUS, the guide recommends a parallel Schottky in the body-diode direction to reduce handover drop [2, §4.5; 4, §5.5].

### Flash Memory

- **RP2040/RP2350 need external flash for standalone nonvolatile program storage; RP2354 includes 2 MB.** Both current minimal designs use W25Q128JVS (128 Mbit = 16 MB, the supported maximum); RP2040 also boots from ROM USB without a flash [1, pp.8-9; 3, p.10].
- **Flash must support 03h serial read with 24-bit addressing.** Also needs 02h 256-byte page program, 05h status read, 06h WREN, and 20h 4 kB sector erase for programming via bootrom [1, §2.6; 3, §3.3].
- **Boot probe runs at ~1 MHz, cycling all 4 CPOL/CPHA combinations** looking for valid CRC32 in second stage. Use a compatible second-stage/XIP configuration; continuous-read modes must also recover under the ROM reset sequence [1, §2.6; 3, §3.3].
- **Place the BOOTSEL 1 kohm branch close to the flash.** It lets the button pull QSPI_SS low for boot while limiting contention when firmware drives chip select; keep the switch when omitting external flash on RP2354 [1, p.9; 3, p.10].
- **Keep QSPI traces short and direct.** High-frequency bus in regular use -- minimize crosstalk to nearby circuits. SPI flash interface details -> `interfaces/spi.md` [1, §2.2; 3, §3.1].

### RP2350 Secondary Flash / PSRAM

- **RP2350 supports second memory device on same QSPI pins** with GPIO0 as XIP_CS1n. The minimal design selects R10 (QSPI_SS to flash) or R9 (GPIO0 to flash) with 0-ohm links; these reference designators are board-specific [3, §3.2].
- **RP2354 has internal 2 MB flash.** External U3 can be omitted entirely, or used as secondary flash/PSRAM. GPIO0 defaults to pull-low at power-up, so the minimal design's secondary CS# needs a 10 kohm pull-up [3, §3.2].

### USB

- **27 ohm series resistors on USB_DP and USB_DM, placed close to RP2040/RP2350.** Internal PHY handles pull-ups/pull-downs for speed detection. Maximum data rate is full-speed (12 Mbps); low-speed host/device operation is also supported [1, §2.4.1; 3, §5.1].
- **USB routing example: the 1 mm two-layer minimal boards use 0.8 mm traces with a 0.15 mm gap for approximately 90 ohm differential impedance.** Recalculate for the actual stackup; this geometry is not universal [1, §2.4.1; 3, §5.1].
- **Pico/Pico 2 USB host use requires 5V at the board VBUS pin.** VSYS alone does not supply downstream USB power; this is a board power-path requirement, not a 5V MCU pin [2, §4.5; 4, §5.5].

### Crystal Oscillator

- **12 MHz crystal: Abracon ABM8-272-T3, 15 pF load caps, 1 kohm series resistor on XOUT (Rs).** This circuit is tuned for 3.3V IOVDD. With the guide's assumed 3 pF parasitics, 15 pF / 2 + 3 pF = 10.5 pF, approximately the crystal's 10 pF CL [1, §2.3; 3, §4].
- **Rs = 1 kohm prevents crystal overdrive at 3.3V.** If IOVDD < 3.3V, drive current drops -- crystal amplitude may be too low or fail to start. The guide calls for a smaller Rs and re-testing the entire circuit across temperature [1, §2.3; 3, §4].
- **RP2350 can run without external crystal** (internal oscillator), but frequency is not well controlled -- varies with process, voltage, and temperature. USB needs an accurate external source: the reference uses a 12 MHz crystal, but a suitable CMOS clock into XIN is also supported [3, §4].
- Crystal selection and load cap calculation details -> `misc/crystal.md`.

### ADC

- **No on-chip voltage reference.** ADC uses its own supply as reference. On Pico: ADC_AVDD filtered from 3.3V via 201 ohm + 2.2 uF RC filter. Supply/reference drop is about 30 mV (150 uA * 200 ohm), not a universal ADC conversion-offset specification. That current estimate excludes the temperature sensor and varies by chip, temperature and sampling [2, §4.3].
- **For better accuracy: LM4040 3.0V shunt reference** from ADC_VREF to ground. Limits ADC range to 0-3.0V. About 1.5 mA flows through the 200 ohm feed resistor at nominal 3.3V; this includes ADC current, so it is not all shunt current [2, §4.3].
- **On Pico/Pico 2, force the board SMPS into PWM mode (GPIO23 high) during ADC measurements.** PFM mode causes significant ripple at light load. Re-enable PFM between measurements to save power [2, §4.3; 4, §5.3].
- **ADC GPIO26-29 have reverse diode to VDDIO.** Input must not exceed VDDIO + 300 mV. When RP2040 is unpowered, voltage on these pins leaks into VDDIO rail. Digital GPIO0-25 are failsafe up to 3.63V when unpowered [2, §4.2; 5, p.3].

### GPIO and Pin Behavior

- **RP2040 digital GPIO0-25 have no pulls when powered off.** Account for the resulting high-impedance state in external circuitry; the 3.63V power-off tolerance does not make these 5V-tolerant [5, p.3].
- **RP2350B (QFN-80): 48 GPIO, 8 ADC channels.** QFN-80 board is larger to accommodate extra header pins. GPIO and power mapping differs from QFN-60 -- do not assume pin compatibility [3, pp.4-5].

### Low Power Design

- **RP2040 power-switching app note quotes ~180 uA typical in deep sleep, strongly dependent on process, voltage and temperature.** Treat this as the app note's estimate, not a guaranteed minimum or maximum; use its power-gating circuit for lower standby [5, p.2].
- **Power switching circuit for near-zero standby:** Pushbutton charges cap through R1, raising regulator EN. RP2040 boots, latches PWRCTL GPIO high to hold power. To power off, float PWRCTL -- cap discharges through R3, EN drops. Use the app note's DMG1012T (low Vt N-FET with ESD clamp) for button sense; these are example circuits requiring application-specific build verification [5, pp.3-7].
- **Pico 2 typical board current at 5V VBUS: P1.0 = 233 uA, P1.7 = 148 uA, RUN low = 388 uA.** These are not chip limits: exposed GPIO/SWD/SWCLK are unconnected, USB PHY is powered down with DP/DM pull-downs enabled, and the USB cable remains attached to a host [4, §4.1.1].
- **Pico/Pico 2 board SMPS power-save mode: GPIO23 low (default).** PFM mode at light load saves considerable power but increases ripple. GPIO23 high forces PWM mode -- lower ripple, worse light-load efficiency [2, §4.4; 4, §5.4].
- Crystal load cap and power calculations -> `misc/crystal.md`, `power/switching.md`.

## Common Mistakes

- **A substitute crystal with matching headline specs still needs temperature testing.** Raspberry Pi warns that equivalent specifications alone do not establish stability of the tuned circuit [1, §2.3.1].
- **RP2350 producing 1.1V at light load does not validate its regulator layout.** Raspberry Pi found that a reversed inductor couples into C7 and upsets regulation; its compact layout and marked Abracon inductor address that failure [3, pp.6-8].
- **Connecting battery directly to VSYS without Schottky diode or P-FET.** If USB is also connected, VBUS back-feeds into battery -- potentially dangerous with unprotected Li-ion cells. Use the reference diode or P-FET power path between battery and VSYS [2, §4.5; 4, §5.5].
- **Resetting the MCU without power-cycling flash can strand flash in continuous-read mode.** Verify that the selected flash exits that mode under the ROM recovery sequence; otherwise retain command-prefixed reads [1, §2.6; 3, §3.3].
- **External ADC signals can back-power an unpowered RP2040 through GPIO26-29.** Isolate those inputs when the MCU supply is switched off; the digital-pin failsafe rule does not cover them [2, §4.2; 5, p.3].
